# TTRPG Space Tracker
## The Vision:
Tracking resources in ttrpg's is generally regarded as the opposite of fun. For this reason, many adventuring parties have decided to ignore most resource tracking (like keeping track of how many arrows you have). 
The Daggerheart group that I play in decided that we wanted to do a campaign in space, with theming and pacing like Firefly and a few others of our favorite space media. Because of this, we decided that resource tracking
is a pretty important part about space travel, one that would be a big part of our lives on the ship. However, we understand that tracking resources manually is both tedious and not very fun. I realized that I could just
make a full application for us to use instead.

`NOTE: THIS PROJECT IS UNDER DEVELOPMENT FOR PERSONAL USAGE. WHILE IT WILL LIKELY BECOME POLISHED ENOUGH FOR PUBLIC USAGE IT WILL TAKE A WHILE.`

`NOTE #2: I realized that Daggerheart's rule documentation is in a fairly consistent pattern on their website and would likely be easy to web scrape. I may consider making a character sheet builder and tracker partway 
through this project, to make all of our lives just a little easier`
